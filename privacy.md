<div class="academic-page">
  <p class="lede">
    This is a personal academic website. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or any other tracking,
    and it does not ask you for personal information.
  </p>

  <h2>What happens when you visit</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>
      <strong>Hosting.</strong> The site is hosted on <a href="https://pages.github.com/">GitHub Pages</a>.
      Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address when your browser requests a page and may keep it in
      its server logs for security purposes. See the
      <a href="https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement">GitHub General Privacy Statement</a>.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Fonts and icons</strong> are served from this website itself, not from Google Fonts or other
      third-party servers.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Light or dark theme.</strong> If you use the button in the menu bar to change the colour theme,
      your choice is saved in your own browser (local storage) so that the site remembers it on your next visit.
      It is never sent anywhere. You can delete it by clearing your browser's site data.
    </li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Links and embedded content</h2>
  <p>
    Links to other websites (journals, GitHub, OSF, social media and so on) take you to services with their own
    privacy policies. Two older blog posts embed interactive apps hosted on
    <a href="https://www.shinyapps.io/">shinyapps.io</a> (Posit), which load from Posit's servers when you open
    those posts.
  </p>

  </p>
